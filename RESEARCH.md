# Research

Cross-project research and reusable lessons from this project are cataloged in the central private research library:

**`DumpsterFireWorks/Projects-research--library-`**

Use the library to discover research, architecture investigations, product evolution, experiments, audits, and reusable findings across the Applied Intelligence family.

## Authority boundary

- This repository remains authoritative for its current code/status and original artifacts.
- The research library is the cross-project discovery and preservation index. It does not change project authority or activate implementation.
- Original source paths and commits remain authoritative provenance.

## Cleanup rule

Before archiving or deleting this repository or any research-bearing branch, issue, PR, experiment, or document, verify that reusable research has been cataloged in `Projects-research--library-`.

Do not move credentials, personal information, private runtime data, or sensitive evidence into the research library.
